# Reflection: fedml-ai_fedml

## Inactivity patterns
Overall activity pattern: **irregular**. The project's longest gap ran from 2022-01 to 2022-02 (2 months), and its timeline has 0 gap(s) of at least three months. Its current status is **Inactive** (last GitHub commit: 2024-05-13).

## Interpreting the longest gap
Fairly easy. The gap lines up with the December holidays, and the new names afterward show fresh contributors restarting the work.

## Likely reasons for inactivity and recovery
**Why it went inactive:** Short 2-month gap over the Dec 2021 holiday period, after a burst of research contributions (fedopt fixes, VFL experiments).

**Recovery:** Yes. New contributors arrived in Mar 2022 adding features (median aggregation, scheduler, cross-silo module) as the project moved toward a broader open-source platform. Who drove it: New contributors (bangawayoo, chaoyanghe, Sunwoo Lee, alex.liang).

## Summary
A large research platform with many rotating contributors and bursty activity. The gap was brief and ended by new people, but GitHub shows no commits since May 2024, so it is now inactive.
