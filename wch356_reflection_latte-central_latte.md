# Reflection: latte-central_latte

## Inactivity patterns
Overall activity pattern: **cyclical**. The project's longest gap ran from 2021-02 to 2022-10 (21 months), and its timeline has 9 gap(s) of at least three months. Its current status is **Active** (last GitHub commit: 2026-05-29).

## Interpreting the longest gap
Hard. The gap is 21 months long and the last commit before it only says beta10 was starting, so nothing in the commits explains the long pause.

## Likely reasons for inactivity and recovery
**Why it went inactive:** Long 21-month gap after starting beta10 in Jan 2021; a single-maintainer academic project, so likely paused due to the maintainer's other commitments.

**Recovery:** Yes. Maintainer returned in Nov 2022 to build a new search facility (pattern matcher), then bumped versions. Who drove it: Same maintainer (Frederic Peschanski).

## Summary
A single-maintainer project with cyclical bursts of work separated by long pauses. The longest gap was hard to explain from commits alone and ended with a new feature.
